Privacy Policy

App: AlwaysInTouch (pl.haskelar.alwaysintouch)
Last updated: 19 August 2026

In short. AlwaysInTouch is a serverless messenger. Conversations, photos, map pins and game scores never pass through our infrastructure, because there is no such infrastructure. Devices connect directly to one another — over a local network created by one of the phones' hotspots, or over Bluetooth Low Energy.

We do not operate user accounts. We do not know your name, e-mail address or phone number, because we never ask for them.

1. Data that stays on your device

The following data is stored locally only, in a database encrypted with SQLCipher whose key is held in the Android system keystore:

  • the content of text messages,
  • photos sent to and received from the group,
  • the display name you choose on first launch,
  • map pins and notes,
  • game scores,
  • Guide conversations — your questions, the answers, and the trimmed source passages they were built from,
  • the diagnostic log (technical connection information — see §5).

Guide conversations survive leaving a group — they are not part of a session, and ending one does not erase them. You delete them the same way as everything else: by uninstalling the app or clearing its data.

Separately, outside that database, the app's folder holds the material you downloaded: offline maps, the Guide's knowledge packs and its model. That is content you fetched, not data about you — none of it is produced by your use of the app.

Android backup is disabled for this app (allowBackup="false"), so this data is never copied to Google Drive.

Uninstalling the app deletes it irreversibly. We hold no copy of it and cannot recover it.

2. Data shared with other group members

When you join a group, its other members receive — directly from your device to theirs — whatever you send them:

  • messages and photos,
  • your display name,
  • your position on the group map, if you enable location sharing,
  • map pins and game scores.

Location sharing has no default state and no continuous mode. Every share is a separate, explicit decision: you choose how often your position is sent and for how long — four hours at the most — after which it expires by itself. Positions are never written to storage on any device: neither yours on other people's phones, nor theirs on yours. They live in the memory of the running app and disappear with the session.

Traffic between devices is encrypted. It passes through no intermediary server and does not travel over the internet — it stays within the local network or the Bluetooth link.

Group members are people you invite yourself, or whose invitation you accept. We have no visibility into who is in a group or what is exchanged within it.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionWhat it is used for

Camera Scanning the QR code when joining a group, and taking photos to send to the group.
Bluetooth (scan, advertise, connect) Discovering nearby devices and handing over network details when joining a group or restoring a dropped connection.
Wi-Fi and nearby devices Starting the hotspot on the device hosting the session, and letting the other devices join it.
Notifications Informing you about new messages and about the session state.
Foreground service / battery optimisation exemption Keeping the connection to the group alive while the screen is off. Without it the system suspends the process and the group loses connectivity.
Attached radio board (USB) Long-range mode, if you attach a board of your own. The system asks for access to that specific device when you plug it in. With no board attached, the permission is used for nothing.
Internet Only the uses listed in §4.

Your location is never sent to us or to any third party. It goes only to the members of your group, and only when you enable location sharing yourself.

4. The only internet connections

The app is designed to work without an internet connection. It reaches the network in five situations, all of them typically before you set off:

  1. RevenueCat — handling the purchase and verifying an active Pro subscription. RevenueCat receives an anonymous device identifier (in the form $RCAnonymousID:…), purchase details and standard technical connection data. We do not send it your name, your conversations or your location. RevenueCat's privacy policy: revenuecat.com/privacy
  2. Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — searching for places in the offline map wizard. The search term you type is sent. osmfoundation.org
  3. Map tile server — downloading the offline map package for the area you select.
  4. packs.haskelar.pl (Cloudflare R2, our own address) — downloading the catalogue of material and whatever you pick from it: the Guide's knowledge packs and its model. The server receives standard connection data (IP address, request headers) and which file you are downloading. We do not send it your name, your conversations, your questions to the Guide, or your location. The catalogue is signed with the publisher's key and the app rejects it if the signature does not match.
  5. Google Play services (ML Kit) — downloading the translator's language packs. The download is carried out by Google on your device; Google receives which language pack you are downloading, along with the standard data of your Google account and device. The translation itself then happens on the phone alone — the text being translated is never sent anywhere. policies.google.com/privacy

Outside these five cases the app sends nothing to the internet. In particular, it contains no advertising and no analytics tooling.

5. Diagnostic log

The app keeps a local technical log (connection state, errors) that helps diagnose connectivity problems. It lives in the app's directory on your device and is never sent anywhere automatically.

You can export it and send it to us yourself from the Diagnostics screen — this happens solely on your initiative and under your control. The log may contain Wi-Fi network names and participants' display names; it does not contain message content.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data.

7. Your rights and data deletion

Because we operate no user accounts and store none of your data on servers, there is no account to delete and no dataset for which a copy could be requested.

You delete local data by uninstalling the app or clearing its data in the system settings.

Purchase data is held by RevenueCat and in your Google Play account — for those matters, refer to the policies of those services.

8. Changes to this policy

We will announce material changes through the update notes on Google Play. The date of the most recent change appears at the top of this document.
